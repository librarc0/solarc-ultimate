# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

SolArc-Ultimate welcomes contributors who discuss problems directly, respectfully,
and with attention to evidence. The project is open to users adapting the system
for ultimate frisbee and other rating or team-management scenarios.

## Expected Behavior

- Be clear and specific when reporting bugs or proposing changes.
- Respect different experience levels and deployment environments.
- Avoid sharing private player data, credentials, production URLs, or team
  records in public issues.
- Keep disagreement focused on the technical decision, not the person.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private credentials or personal data.
- Repeated off-topic posting or spam.
- Misrepresenting this project as an official Microsoft TrueSkill
  implementation.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content that violates this
code, and may restrict participation when needed to protect users and data.
